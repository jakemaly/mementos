# Notes

Welcome to my life notes. Five domains, one flat system.

## Domains

- [[school/index]] — Academic work, organized by course
- [[recruiting/index]] — Internship and job recruiting
- [[freelance/index]] — Client work and projects
- [[side-projects/index]] — Personal projects (active and ideas)
- [[hobbies/index]] — Reading, fitness, creative pursuits

## Quick Capture

Need to capture something fast? Create a file directly in `notes/` with a
descriptive name, then move it to the right domain later. This simulates
an inbox without adding a top-level folder.
