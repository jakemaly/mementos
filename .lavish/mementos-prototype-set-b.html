What did we learn about keeping local-first search useful for a small team?

Archive
Answer readyGrounded in 3 selected sources

The durable pattern is to keep the source of truth portable, make the index replaceable, and agree on a small review habit. That lets a team share useful retrieval without turning the archive into a locked service [01]. The tradeoff is operational: someone still owns sync boundaries and cleanup [02], while hosted search can remain a convenience rather than the only copy [03].
